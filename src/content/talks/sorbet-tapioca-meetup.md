A meetup talk walking through how to introduce Sorbet and Tapioca into a
real-world Rails application without grinding development to a halt.

## Abstract

Type checking in Ruby used to feel like a pipe dream. With Sorbet and Tapioca,
it's now genuinely practical to add gradual typing to an existing codebase. This
talk covers where to start, how to generate RBI files for your gems, and how to
ratchet up strictness over time.
